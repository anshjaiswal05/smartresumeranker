🔒 Privacy & Storage Architecture

Zero File Retention: Smart Resume Ranker adheres strictly to a zero-retention policy for uploaded original documents. Your uploaded PDF, DOC, and DOCX files are temporarily held in server memory for text extraction and scoring, then immediately deleted.

Only structured metrics (ATS scores, detected skill names, and generated interview history) are stored in Firestore to power your dashboard.
